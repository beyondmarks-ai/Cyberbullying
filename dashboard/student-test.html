Voluntary classroom test

See the IP used to visit this page

This exercise shows what a website can observe when you connect to it. It does not obtain your IP from an Instagram message.

After you agree, this page checks your connection every 15 seconds while visible, for up to one hour. Your instructor sees your initial IP and any changed IP, your entered username and the time. The username is not verified with Instagram. No password or location is requested.

On classroom Wi-Fi, this may be your phone's local IP. Public IPs may be shared or belong to a VPN. Records are kept for up to 24 hours while the dashboard is running, and the instructor can clear them sooner.

Automatic checks are off.

Checks pause when this page is hidden or your phone suspends it. Changing networks works only if the server is still reachable. This local classroom link usually cannot be reached on mobile data.

You can close this page to decline. Opening it alone does not save a test record.
